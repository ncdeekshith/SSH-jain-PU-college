Information

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Information we collect

When a student or parent submits an admission enquiry, the college may collect the student name, contact number, preferred stream and any message voluntarily provided.

How information is used

Information is used only to respond to enquiries, explain admissions, communicate academic information and support the application process.

Storage and sharing

Enquiry information may be stored in a college-controlled Google Sheet or equivalent administrative system. The college does not sell personal information.

Data choices

Students and parents may contact the college to request correction or removal of enquiry information, subject to applicable institutional and legal requirements.

Website data

The website may use essential technical data required for reliable delivery. Any future analytics or third-party services should be disclosed here before activation.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the college administration at A.D.B. Campus, Harapanahalli, Vijayanagara District, Karnataka.
